---
name: workspace
description: Where your Commercializer workspace stands — your company and its competitors, what is kept about them, the watches running and the workflows your plan runs. Use when the person asks what Commercializer knows, what is kept, or where to start.
---

Lay out the person's Commercializer workspace in one screen with the Commercializer tools.

1. `list_profiles`: the companies the workspace holds (`workspace: true`), each with its link; an `archived` one reads
   but runs nothing.
2. `get_profile` on each of them (on the one the person names, when they name one): what it makes, its markets,
   products and goals, the competitors it tracks, and every kept finding with its sources and age; say which are stale.
3. `list_watches` on each: every watch, its cadence and last run.
4. `list_workflows` on one of them: the ones the plan runs, grouped by pack, one line each.

Write short sections with plain headings and keep every `[read …]` id a finding carries. End with the one thing you would
do next (a workflow to run, a stale finding to read again, a competitor to add) and offer to do it.
