description: Where your Commercializer workspace stands — your company and its competitors, what is kept about them, the watches running and the workflows the plan runs.
---

Read the person's Commercializer workspace through the `commercializer` MCP server and lay it out in one screen:

1. `profiles` — the primary company and the competitors tracked, each with its link.
2. `profile` on the primary — what it makes, markets, products, goals, and every kept finding with its sources and age;
   flag any marked stale.
3. `watches` on the primary — each watch, its cadence and last run.
4. `workflows` on the primary — the ones the plan runs now, grouped by pack, one line each.

Write it as short sections with plain headings; keep every `[read …]` id where a finding carries one. End with the one
thing you would do next (a workflow to run, a stale finding to re-read, a competitor to add) and offer to do it.
